* P2P network of mining nodes

** Build a sharechain for transparent accounting

A blockchain of shares mined by miners under pool's difficulty target.

All shares are weak blocks, i.e. much lower difficulty than current
bitcoin difficulty.

** Pay through coinbase

Miners are paid for their shares appearing in the PPLNS window. [Add
simple clean lines diagram]

Top miners are paid directly from the coinbase. Smaller miners sell
their shares to the larger miners. [Add simple clean lines diagram]

A hashrate market where any buyer of shares can earn enough shares to
get a payout of virgin coins directly from the coinbase. Miners need
to sell shares before they fall out of the PPLNS window. The market
does price discovery for shares as expiring options.
